Keep original independent data immutable and separate from the adjudicated interpretation. Calculate predeclared agreement/reliability before adjudication, then retain both sets. Never resolve a disagreement by silently overwriting the original trace, deleting an unfavorable case, excluding a slower reader or converting an unobserved opportunity into a zero.

A material authority/safety error, harmful misconception, invalid measurement or inaccessible required path holds the affected claim. If reliability is weak or its interval too broad, revise and recalibrate. Record a remaining disagreement plainly; consensus is not proof that the scenario models a real incident correctly.
