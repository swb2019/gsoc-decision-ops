# Formative observer sheet

Blank per-person template for the separately authorized ten-user formative review. Use the [shared header](README.md#shared-record-header). Participant label: ______. Observer label: ______. Intended perspective: analyst / supervisor / leader / other described context ______. Consent reference held outside the app: ______. Accessibility supports requested and supplied: ______. Session stop/withdrawal conditions communicated: ______.

Record no employer incidents, real sites, names or credentials. No participant has been recruited by this template. Keep identifying records outside the public project. Accessibility support is not inferior performance; distinguish it from substantive coaching or facilitator rescue.

## Observer conduct

Start from the agreed entry route and a fresh candidate session. Do not show calibration answer context or a demonstration first. Record the requested mode and help rather than imposing a faster mode. Use the participant's ordinary reading pace. If help is requested, record the exact intervention and when it occurred; later assisted completion must not be labeled unassisted. Stop or provide needed support for access barriers and record the barrier rather than excluding the person.

Neutral opening: “Please use this fictional exercise as you normally would. You can read, pause or stop. I am observing the product, not evaluating you for employment. Tell me when you are unsure what to do.”

Do not hint at a preferred control or reward replay. Ask the following prompts only at their recorded point, after observing the action where possible.

| Point                                               | Neutral prompt                                                                                                            | Response, observation and timestamp |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Initial orientation                                 | “What do you understand your role to be, and what would you do first?”                                                    |                                     |
| After first consequence                             | “What changed after your choice? What in the record helps you explain that?”                                              |                                     |
| After a constrained commitment or dependency change | “What changed here, and what does that mean for the available options?”                                                   |                                     |
| At review                                           | “Choose a finding and show me the evidence behind it. What do the business outcomes or numbers in this report represent?” |                                     |
| After review                                        | “What other approach, if any, would you like to test, and why?”                                                           |                                     |
| Equal finish/replay offer                           | “You may finish here or try another approach. Either is useful to us.”                                                    |                                     |
| If replay is chosen                                 | “Use the comparison to inspect the next consequence. What is similar or different?”                                       |                                     |
| At exit                                             | “What was confusing, repetitive, useful or missing? What influenced your choice to continue or stop?”                     |                                     |
