# Hourglass capabilities and evidence

Glasshouse is a synthetic educational practice product. Its implementation exposes decision records and modeled consequences for reflection. Independent rubric calibration, first-use/comprehension studies and real-world transfer have not been established. The public-release qualification record remains on hold.

## Feedback interpretation

A structured rule can identify that a cited observation was already corrected, approval remains pending, a resource is committed, a verification completed or a handoff is missing. These findings identify their rule version and evidence events. “Not observed” is distinct from an observed omission. Rationale, uncertainty interpretation, proportionality, business reasoning and communication quality remain for human review. Keyword presence, prose length, rapid clicking and favorable outcomes receive no quality credit.

Completed means the authored terminal and handoff conditions were met. It does not establish mastery, retained learning, leadership potential, employability or operational readiness. Immediate replay does not establish retention. A modeled alternative is a scenario-dependent calculation, not a prediction of a real incident.

## Correction to the legacy methodology

The previous methodology described textbook-faithful coverage and mathematical soundness more strongly than the available evidence justified. Those claims are withdrawn. Legacy grade/point records do not become Glasshouse assessment results. Raster legacy PDF exports remain visual reports and are not relabeled accessible. Legacy partial sessions lack the complete state needed for faithful recovery and are preserved as read-only records.

Foundation corrections remove financial activity multipliers, preserve exact selected treatments, repair full-range seed encoding and authored event order, require explicit queue acknowledgment, decouple pre-action threat from selected posture, and make review read-only. The fixture of USD 90,000 avoided loss and USD 20,000 cost yields USD 70,000 net benefit and 350% ROI. Zero-cost ROI is not applicable.

## Sources and qualifications

The PRD draws on [FEMA HSEEP exercise evaluation](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/hseep-resources/eegs), [NIST SP 800-61 revision 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final), [ODNI ICD 203](https://archive.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf), and [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). These inform design intentions; no framework endorsement, equivalence or training effect is inferred. Their implementation mappings remain subject to independent review.

The product aims for an approachable 18–20-minute flagship and a meaningful choice within 90 seconds. Those are proposed experience targets, not measured results. Three independent reviewers and ten formative participants must establish the PRD’s content, comprehension and accessibility gates before public qualification. A later consented learning-signal study is a separate authorization and does not follow automatically from engagement.
