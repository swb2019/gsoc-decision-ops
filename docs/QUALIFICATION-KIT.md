## Learning study and expansion

G3's separately authorized 24-adult learning-signal pilot remains prospective: randomized Hourglass versus a time-matched active text/worked-example condition; practice days 0/3/7; unfamiliar neutral assessment at baseline/day14/day28; two blinded raters and a third adjudicator. Balance prior experience/baseline performance and counterbalance three reviewed parallel assessment forms. Match objectives, facts, exposure and feedback opportunity while recording remaining differences. Assessment excludes substantive coaching while retaining equivalent accessibility supports. Freeze allocation, forms, scoring, exclusions, missingness/attrition, analysis, consent and retention policy before enrollment.

The primary signal is the baseline-adjusted day14 between-group difference; secondary signals include day28 retention, critical-task misses, uncertainty handling and confidence calibration where supported by the task. Participants are the experimental units; multiple decisions are not additional people. Report intervals, individual distributions, assistance, attrition and sensitivity analyses. A proposed useful-effect threshold is 10 points on the common percentage scale. An interval spanning no benefit remains inconclusive for efficacy.

The prospective feasibility gate is at least 20/24 completing day28 and valid retrievable records for at least 95% of completed assessments. A proposed larger-validation investment gate combines passed usability/reliability, a day14 estimate of at least +10 points, a 95% interval excluding deterioration worse than 5 points, and no material harmful misconception; it does not establish efficacy if the interval includes no benefit. Twenty-four volunteers are a feasibility/signal pilot, not adequately powered proof by default. Pause assessment for unreliable measurement, material harmful misconceptions/scenario defects or an inaccessible required path. Low retention or differential attrition triggers explanation/redesign, not selective deletion.

Recruitment, employer involvement, compensation and collection need their own authorized plan. Keep voluntary participation separate from employment appraisal and identities/consent outside the game. Delete raw identifiable pilot data 30 days after agreed analysis completion unless participants explicitly consent otherwise. These are prospective protocol fields, not completed approvals or results.

The engineering traces are familiar Glasshouse variants, not unfamiliar transfer assessments. Additional travel/facility cases, spaced return paths and the facilitated local-session edition follow flagship qualification. Networked roles, enterprise/general authoring and AI experiments remain separately gated G4 decisions. No participant contact, study result or efficacy is implied by this preparation.
