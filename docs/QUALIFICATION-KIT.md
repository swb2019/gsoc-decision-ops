# Glasshouse qualification packet

The reviewable engineering candidate is ready for qualification work. This packet does not authorize contacting participants, collecting research data or spending money. The accountable product owner is Shannon Brown. Assign named human executors/reviewers and record the exact candidate revision before starting each gate.

## Independent scenario and rubric review

Recruitment and participation need the owner’s separate authorization. The requested reviewer mix is one physical-security/GSOC practitioner, one cyber incident responder and one exercise/learning specialist. Preserve independent ratings before adjudication. Software checks and AI reviews do not substitute for these people.

Review twelve authored vignettes covering: verified narrow fault with continued service; verified narrow fault with bounded manual service; scoped compromise with approved isolation; constrained guard capacity; delayed owner with delegated fallback; current but nonthreatening image; stale-image correction and an update to its earlier recipient; unsupported emergency pause; unavailable approval; lucky unsupported monitoring; adverse validation after a sound plan; and early versus controlled handoff. Then use a separate held-out trace set for calibration, including novel reasonable alternatives.

For each exposed task record opportunity, available evidence, action, consequence, event IDs, eight dimension anchors and material disputes. Interpretive text remains human-scored; the application’s automatic reference/procedure checks are not substitutes. Resolve authority/safety disputes before publication. Target at least 90% exact agreement on critical flags. A research reliability index and interval require a suitable prespecified rater design; do not manufacture a statistic from development fixtures.

## Ten-participant formative protocol

Use intended analyst, supervisor and leader perspectives, including accessibility accommodations. Obtain purpose-limited consent before any separately approved observation. Keep identity/consent outside the app and never treat the session as employment appraisal. No account or cloud transmission is necessary for public solo play.

Observe role/first action within 90 seconds; a visible consequence within three active minutes; complete case plus review within 25 minutes without rescue; explanation of one evidence/resource/authority tradeoff; ability to find a finding’s evidence within 30 seconds; and an independently chosen alternative to test. Record mode, help, reading accommodations, active time, simulated time, problems and reasons for stopping. Preserve every failure rather than removing slower readers to improve results.

Offer Finish and Try another approach equally. Record voluntary replay without incentive or pressure. Proposed gate: at least eight of ten meet the comprehension/task targets, all ten have a usable supported path, and at least five voluntarily replay. These are formative product signals, not population estimates or evidence of transfer.

## Device and accessibility matrix

Record exact OS/browser/build and hardware before the run. Required physical tiers: a supported integrated-graphics desktop, a mid-range Android handset and an iPhone/Safari device. Record five cold-load measurements per route/device; label local lab latency separately from field INP. Run a full mission and review with NVDA and with VoiceOver/Safari, keyboard only, 320px and 390px layouts, 200% text and 400% zoom, motion disabled and audio disabled. Check visible focus and return focus, headings/labels, 44px primary targets, announcements and all exports.

Compare the same route/coverage/commitment explanation using schematic and optional 3D. Inspect quiet handover, constrained operation and causal review stills/motion. Retain 3D only if it improves comprehension or engagement without reducing task success. Verify GPU/context loss, sustained jank, failed optional assets and local speech unavailability preserve play. Run ten load/exit cycles and inspect memory growth. Automated axe checks supplement this matrix; they do not establish conformance.

## Learning study and expansion

The PRD’s 24-person day-0/3/7 practice and day-14/28 unfamiliar-assessment study remains a separate G2–G3 activity. Before enrollment, approve recruitment, consent, compensation/cost cap, allocation, blinded raters, forms, scoring, missingness, exclusions, analysis and deletion policy.
