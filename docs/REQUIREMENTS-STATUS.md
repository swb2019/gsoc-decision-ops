# Requirement implementation status

This is an engineering trace for the next-generation Glasshouse candidate, not a declaration that G0–G2 are qualified. The authoritative publication decision and exact candidate revision belong in [release/qualification.json](../release/qualification.json). The source PRD remains private; the summaries below describe the implementation without reproducing it.

Accountable owner: **Shannon Brown**. Executor: **AI-assisted engineering under Shannon’s direction**. Independent human reviewers: **pending assignment** across physical security/GSOC, cyber response and exercise/learning design. AI review and automated checks are engineering evidence only.

Status meanings:

- **E — Implemented engineering candidate:** a concrete mechanism and executable checks exist. Release-wide human gates still apply.
- **P — Partial; qualification pending:** the row identifies incomplete qualification, validation, or a remaining bounded implementation gap. It is not silently moved to a later gate.
- **D — G3/G4 deferred:** the PRD deliberately conditions this expansion on earlier evidence and a separate decision.

The [design note](GLASSHOUSE-DESIGN.md) records dependencies, smaller alternatives and removal conditions. The [qualification packet](QUALIFICATION-KIT.md) defines the human work still required. Cross-cutting deletion rule: remove a mechanism if it cannot support its stated practice objective without undermining evidence integrity, accessible play or the declared local-data boundary.

## Open acceptance work

The remaining human gates are real work, not software defects to be bypassed: independent content/rubric calibration, ten-user formative evaluation, named assistive-technology/device/performance checks, the art/audio comparator, source/license review and a staged public rollback exercise. Final automated evidence must be regenerated for the exact candidate after concurrent repair work. Until the qualification record changes with named evidence, **public release remains on hold**.

G3/G4 rows are intentionally deferred by the product sequence. They do not justify dropping a G0–G2 failure or converting an incomplete engineering flow into a human-validation issue.
