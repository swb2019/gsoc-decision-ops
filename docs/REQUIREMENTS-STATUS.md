# Requirement implementation status

This is an engineering trace for the next-generation Glasshouse candidate, not a declaration that G0–G2 are qualified. The authoritative publication decision and exact candidate revision belong in [release/qualification.json](../release/qualification.json). The source PRD remains private; the summaries below describe the implementation without reproducing it.

Accountable owner: **Shannon Brown**. Executor: **AI-assisted engineering under Shannon’s direction**. Independent human reviewers: **pending assignment** across physical security/GSOC, cyber response and exercise/learning design. AI review and automated checks are engineering evidence only.

Status meanings:

- **E — Implemented engineering candidate:** a concrete mechanism and executable checks exist. Release-wide human gates still apply.
- **P — Partial; qualification pending:** the row identifies incomplete qualification, validation, or a remaining bounded implementation gap. It is not silently moved to a later gate.
- **D — G3/G4 deferred:** the PRD deliberately conditions this expansion on earlier evidence and a separate decision.

The [design note](GLASSHOUSE-DESIGN.md) records dependencies, smaller alternatives and removal conditions. The [qualification packet](QUALIFICATION-KIT.md) and [blank review materials](../release/review-materials/README.md) define the remaining human work and one consolidated owner-review queue. The [20-trace manifest](../qa-output/calibration/generation-manifest.json) records the current generated versions and hashes; any later rules/assets change requires affected regeneration before review. Cross-cutting deletion rule: remove a mechanism if it cannot support its stated practice objective without undermining evidence integrity, accessible play or the declared local-data boundary.

## Open acceptance work

Prepared review records now cover 20 calibration traces, neutral observation, manual accessibility/devices, the art/audio comparator and rollback. Separate audio channels and explicit voluntary abandonment are implemented; both physical Android engineering runs passed their documented automated scope. Six actual application clips and procedural audio samples are ready for comparison. The remaining human gates are real work, not software defects to be bypassed: independent content/rubric calibration, ten-user formative evaluation, named assistive-technology/device/performance checks, the art/audio comparator, source/license review and a staged public rollback exercise. The final automated evidence identifies the exact candidate in the delivery manifest. Until the qualification record changes with named evidence, **public release remains on hold**.

G3/G4 rows are intentionally deferred by the product sequence. They do not justify dropping a G0–G2 failure or converting an incomplete engineering flow into a human-validation issue.
